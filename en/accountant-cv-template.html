Accountant CV Example and Writing Guide

When applying for accounting roles, your CV should clearly show your technical competency and knowledge of regulations. Employers place particular emphasis on certifications and software skills for accounting positions.

State Your Professional Credentials

If you hold a CPA license (or equivalent) or are working toward one, state this clearly near the top of your CV - it's usually one of the first things reviewers check.

List Accounting Software

Include the accounting and ERP software you use - QuickBooks, SAP, NetSuite, etc. - in a dedicated section, noting which modules you have experience with.

Describe Your Experience With Concrete Data

Back up the number of accounts you managed, the scope of financial statements you prepared, or audit processes you took part in with figures.

  • Example: "Managed monthly bookkeeping for 50+ client accounts."
  • Example: "Developed a checklist that reduced year-end closing time by 20%."

Highlight Your Regulatory Knowledge

Stating up-to-date knowledge of tax regulations, IFRS, or relevant compliance requirements builds confidence with employers.

Conclusion
