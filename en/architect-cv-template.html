Architect CV Example and Writing Guide

🚀 Edit This Template on MobilCV

When applying for architecture roles, your CV should present your design skills and completed projects in a balanced mix of visuals and text. A portfolio link plays a critical role in how architecture firms evaluate CVs.

Always Include Your Portfolio Link

Share a link to your personal website or a platform like Behance/Issuu prominently on your CV. Employers in architecture typically consider the portfolio just as important as the CV itself.

List Your Design Software

Include the software you use - AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, 3ds Max, Lumion, etc. - in a dedicated section. It helps to illustrate how you used these tools on specific projects with examples.

Detail Your Project Experience

Clearly state the scale of projects you've worked on (residential, commercial, urban design, etc.) and your role in each.

  • Example: "Contributed to the schematic design phase of a 500 m² residential project, responsible for material selection."
  • Example: "Conducted site analysis for an urban regeneration project as part of a 3-person team."

Mention Competitions and Awards

If you've entered architecture competitions, won awards, or had projects exhibited, give these their own section - they demonstrate creativity and professional engagement.

Conclusion
