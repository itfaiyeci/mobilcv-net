Truck Driver CV Example and Writing Guide

✨ Fill in this example with your own details on MobilCV: free, no sign-up, works on your phone.

🚀 Create My Truck Driver CV

In driving jobs, employers check your documents before anything else. If your licence category and professional certificates don't match the job, the rest of your CV may not be read - so put them at the top, clearly.

List licences and certificates with dates

Give your licence category (for example C, C+E or the equivalent in your country), professional driver qualifications such as the Driver CPC in the UK and EU, a CDL in the US, any hazardous goods (ADR/HazMat) endorsement and medical checks, with expiry dates.

Describe vehicles and routes

Rigid trucks, articulated lorries, vans or buses are different jobs. Say whether you did local multi-drop delivery, long-haul or international routes.

Lead with safety

Years without accidents, fuel-efficient driving and compliance with driving-hours and tachograph rules signal trust. One concrete sentence is enough.

What Every Truck Driver CV Should Include

  • Licence category and year obtained
  • Professional driver qualification (e.g. CPC, CDL)
  • Medical / fitness checks with validity
  • Vehicle types and routes driven

Example Work Experience Lines

Write your experience in the same concrete, measurable way:

  • "Drove long-haul articulated routes for 5 years with a clean, accident-free record."
  • "Completed around 25 multi-drop deliveries a day on time across the city."
