New Graduate CV Example and Writing Guide

✨ Edit this template on MobilCV - free, no signup, in 1 minute!

🚀 Edit This Template on MobilCV

If your work experience is still limited, your CV should put your academic achievements, internships, and projects front and center. Employers reviewing new graduate CVs look especially for potential and a willingness to learn.

Give Your Education Section Prominence

Since your experience is limited, place your education section near the top of your CV with detail. If your GPA is strong, or you received scholarships or academic honors, include those too.

Highlight Your Internship Experience

Describe your internships in detail - the responsibilities you had and the skills you gained during each one.

  • Example: "Supported the marketing team with data analysis during a 3-month internship."
  • Example: "Played an active role in preparing a client presentation during an internship."

Include University Projects and Club Involvement

Your capstone/final-year project, competitions you participated in, or leadership roles in student clubs are strong assets that can offset limited work experience.

List Your Technical and Soft Skills

Include software you know, languages you speak, and soft skills like communication and teamwork in a dedicated section.

Conclusion
