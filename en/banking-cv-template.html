Banking CV Example and Writing Guide

🚀 Edit This Template on MobilCV

When applying to the banking and finance sector, your CV should clearly demonstrate your analytical skills and client relationship experience. Banks place particular emphasis on reliability and measurable performance in this field.

State Your Financial Certifications

List any relevant licenses, banking certifications, or financial analysis training in a dedicated section - these are often prerequisites for many banking roles.

Show Your Client Portfolio and Sales Performance

State the size of the client portfolio you managed, your cross-selling rates, or your performance against loan/deposit targets using concrete figures.

  • Example: "Managed a retail client portfolio of 150 accounts, increasing cross-sell rate by 30%."
  • Example: "Consistently achieved 110% of monthly loan targets."

Highlight Analytical and Risk Assessment Skills

Back up your experience in financial statement analysis or risk assessment with concrete examples.

Mention Banking Software You've Used

If you're familiar with specific banking systems or financial software, include this under your technical skills.
