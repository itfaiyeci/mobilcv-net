📄 How to Write a CV?

A step-by-step guide with examples and expert tips - exclusive to MobilCV.net

1 Personal Information and Contact Details

This section, at the very top of your CV, is how an employer reaches you. Be sure to include:

  • Full Name – Write it large and legible.
  • Phone – A current, reachable number.
  • Email – Use a professional address (e.g. firstname.lastname@gmail.com).
  • City / Country – State the location you want to work in.
  • LinkedIn or portfolio link – Include it if you have one.
💡 Tip: A photo isn't required in most English-speaking job markets, and is often discouraged in the US and UK for anti-discrimination reasons.

2 Professional Summary

This 3-4 sentence section summarizes who you are. State what you do, what you specialize in, and what you're looking for.

Example:
"Digital marketer with 3 years of experience developing data-driven strategies. Skilled in SEO, social media management, and content marketing. Looking to join an innovative team to grow brand awareness."

3 Work Experience (Chronological Order)

List your most recent job first, working backward. For each role, include:

  • Job title, company name, dates worked
  • A short, focused summary of your responsibilities
  • Back up your achievements with numbers (e.g. "Increased sales by 25%.")
💡 Tip: Internships, volunteer work, and projects can go in this section too.

4 Education

List your school, degree, and dates starting with your most recent. Include any master's degrees, certifications, or bootcamps.

Example:
🎓 University of Istanbul – Business Administration (2018–2022)

5 Skills

List your technical and personal skills. Only include skills you genuinely have.

  • Technical: Programming languages, tools, software.
  • Languages: Your native language and any others you speak (note your level).
  • Personal: Leadership, communication, teamwork, problem-solving.

6 Certifications and Training

Courses, online certifications (Coursera, Udemy, etc.), and job-relevant qualifications belong here.

💡 Tip: Only include certifications relevant to your field. Unrelated ones clutter your CV.

7 References

"Available upon request" is usually sufficient. If you list a reference by name, make sure you have their permission first.

8 Final Check and Formatting

  • Check for spelling and grammar errors.
  • Length: Keep it to 1-2 pages (depending on your experience).
  • Send it as a PDF.
  • File name: FirstnameLastname_CV.pdf (e.g. JaneSmith_CV.pdf).
