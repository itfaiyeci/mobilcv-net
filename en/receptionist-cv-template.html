Receptionist CV Example and Writing Guide

🚀 Create My Receptionist CV

Hotel, clinic or office - the receptionist is the first face of the organisation. Employers look for communication skills, languages and front office system knowledge.

List the systems you use

Hotels use property management systems such as Opera; clinics use appointment systems. List the ones you know along with check-in/check-out, reservations and payments.

Be honest about languages

In hospitality, English is expected and every extra language is an advantage. Don't overstate your level - it will be tested.

Show capacity and guest satisfaction

Mention the size of the hotel, daily arrivals and departures, and times you were named in guest reviews.

What Every Receptionist CV Should Include

  • Front office / booking systems
  • Languages and levels
  • Type and size of property
  • Shift availability

Example Work Experience Lines

Write your experience in the same concrete, measurable way:

  • "Handled around 150 check-ins and check-outs a day in Opera at a 420-room hotel."
  • "Was among the top three staff most mentioned in guest reviews for the season."
