MobilCV for Organisations

Help your clients, students or members create a CV on their phone - free and without signing up. There's no setup, contract or cost for you.

Who is it for?

  • Job centres and employment services
  • Municipal career and social support teams
  • Migrant and refugee organisations, charities and NGOs
  • Vocational courses, adult education centres and schools
  • Career coaches and volunteer mentors

Why MobilCV?

Completely freeNo hidden fees, trial periods or watermarks.
No sign-upUsers don't need to create an email account or password.
No data collectedCV details stay only on the user's own phone - neither we nor your organisation can see them.
Built for phonesPeople without a computer can create a CV in minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Does our organisation have to pay?

No. MobilCV is free for individuals and organisations.

Do we need a contract or to apply?

No. You can put up the poster or share the link. If you like, let us know through the form below - we'd love to hear what your organisation needs.

Can we see how many people use it?

Because we don't collect personal data, we can't provide user-level reports. That's part of the tool's privacy promise.

Can we add our own logo?

We don't offer branded versions at the moment. If you need one, tell us through the form - that's how we measure interest.
